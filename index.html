          where the name comes from

          Meet me at the Lea Rig

          A lea rig is the strip of unploughed land between fields, the bit farmers walked and lived on in the old runrig system. Robert Burns made it a meeting place:

          “I’ll meet thee on the lea-rig, My ain kind dearie O.”Robert Burns, The Lea-Rig (1792)

          Our lea rig is a Victorian corner of Easter Road: chocolate brown outside, warm peach plaster inside, light from two sides. A family-run bakery with American diner pies, Scottish neighbours and a counter that changes every week.
